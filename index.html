About us

Why Durban chooses Principal Plumbers

Principal Plumbers has been keeping Durban's water running since 1999. We're a family-run team of licensed plumbers based in Durban Central, covering everything from a dripping tap in Berea to a full bathroom renovation in Umhlanga. Because we live and work here, our vans are never far away — and our reputation is built on showing up when we say we will.

Every job starts with a clear, fixed quote and ends with work that's guaranteed for 12 months. No hidden fees, no "we'll come back tomorrow", no subcontractors — just qualified plumbers who treat your home like their own. That's why most of our work comes from repeat customers and their neighbours.
